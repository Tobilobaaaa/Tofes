What is enforced

  • Hard: a teacher cannot be in two classes at once; a class cannot take two unrelated subjects at once; no lessons on break; respect “unavailable” teachers.
  • Soft: preferred periods, fewer holes in a class day, at least 6–7 lessons Mon–Thu. Double periods (same subject back-to-back) are welcome.
  • Together: Physics / Accounting / Literature can share a slot, but Literature can be 3 periods while Physics and Accounting are 4 — the extra slot leaves Literature free.
  • Afterward: open the grid, click a lesson, move or swap. Delete old runs from All runs. Lock a cell so the next generate keeps it.
